=== tab3_ablation
\caption{\textbf{Ablations} on test splits (gIoU, \%; N-acc: accuracy on no-target expressions). (a)$\rightarrow$(b): the
MLLM selecting among candidates instead of SAM\,3's own ranking; (b)$\rightarrow$(e): query-conditioned candidates;
(c)$\rightarrow$(e): candidates as mask tokens inside the MLLM instead of pooled features; (d) vs (e): free attention

=== tab4_coverage
\caption{\textbf{Selection versus coverage} on part queries (test splits). \emph{Best in pool}: an oracle that picks the
candidate with the highest IoU. \emph{Gap closed}: share of the distance from SAM\,3's own top instance to the oracle
that \ours{}'s selection recovers. \emph{Better}: queries where one pick beats the other by at least 0.1 IoU. The
candidate pool contains a much better mask than either pick (71.0 vs.\ 49.4 on PARTONOMY), so the remaining errors are
selection errors; most of the best masks come from the query-free point grid, which SAM\,3's text instances alone
would miss.}

=== tab6_editing
\caption{\textbf{Region-grounded editing} on all GEdit-Bench-EN and ImgEdit-Bench samples (CoCoEdit masked protocol).
Each mask source localizes the edit's referent; the editor then edits within that mask (Qwen-Image-Edit: its edit
blended into the mask; FLUX Fill: inpainting the mask). Mask IoU and empty rate (\%) against the GT edit region;
judge: Qwen2.5-VL-72B on the benchmark's scale; LPIPS-out: change outside the GT edit region. The judge favours
